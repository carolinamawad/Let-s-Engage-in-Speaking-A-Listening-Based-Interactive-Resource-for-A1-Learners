📘 About This Resource

This interactive resource is aligned with Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles. It targets sixth-grade students at A1 English proficiency level, focusing on the development of listening comprehension, vocabulary recognition, and guided oral production through interactive, gamified activities.
